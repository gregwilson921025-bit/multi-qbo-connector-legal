Privacy Policy

Multi-Company QuickBooks Connector. Effective October 31, 2026. Published by Gregory Wilson Consulting.

What the connector is

The connector is a Claude Desktop extension that runs on your own computer. It uses your own Intuit developer app keys to read reports from QuickBooks Online companies that you authorize. It does not change, create, or delete anything in QuickBooks.

What we collect

Nothing. Gregory Wilson Consulting does not operate a server for this connector and does not receive, collect, store, or share your data, keys, or tokens.

What stays on your computer

Your Intuit client ID and secret, and the access and refresh tokens Intuit issues when you authorize a company, are kept on your own computer. Tokens are encrypted and stored in your Windows user profile. They are used only to call Intuit's QuickBooks Online API.

Where data goes

Report data is requested directly from Intuit and returned to the Claude Desktop app on your computer, where it is handled under your agreement with Anthropic and the Claude privacy terms. We do not receive a copy.

Disconnecting and deleting

You can disconnect a company at any time with the connector's disconnect tool, which revokes access at Intuit and removes the stored tokens. Uninstalling the extension and deleting the folder %APPDATA%\QuickBooksMulti removes everything stored locally.

Children

The connector is a business tool and is not directed to children.
