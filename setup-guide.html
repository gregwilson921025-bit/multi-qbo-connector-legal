QuickBooks Multi-Company for Claude: Setup and Use Guide

This add-on lets Claude answer questions across all of your QuickBooks Online companies at once, for example "Compare October net income across all my companies." The regular QuickBooks connector in Claude only looks at one company at a time.

What it can and can't do

  • Read-only. It can read reports and look things up. It cannot create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks. That rule is built into the code, not just a setting.
  • Yours alone. It uses your own Intuit developer app and runs on your own computer. Your QuickBooks sign-ins are stored encrypted on this PC, under your Windows login. Nobody else, including the person who gave you this, has access to your books or your keys.
  • You maintain it. This guide covers setup, daily use, reconnecting and updating.

What you need

  • A Windows PC with the Claude desktop app, signed in.
  • An admin login for each QuickBooks Online company you want to connect.
  • About 1 to 2 hours for the one-time Intuit setup in Part 1. After that, connecting each company takes a minute.

Part 1: Your Intuit developer app (one time)

Intuit only lets an app read QuickBooks data if the app is registered with them. You'll register a private app that only you use.

  1. Sign in at https://developer.intuit.com with your QuickBooks login.
  2. Complete your profile. Intuit asks for your address and phone number (My Hub > Account Profile) before it will turn on production (real company) access.
  3. Create the app. Go to My Hub > App dashboard, click the + to create an app, and choose QuickBooks Online. Give it any name, such as "My Company Reports". When it asks for scopes, choose Accounting only (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting).
  4. Get production keys. Open the app and click Get production keys. Intuit walks you through two sections: - App details. Intuit may ask for links such as terms of use and a privacy policy. For a private app used only by your own company, a simple page on your company website or a published document stating that the app is internal and read-only is enough. - Compliance and the App Assessment Questionnaire. This takes about an hour. Answer it truthfully. Facts you'll need:
    • Private app, used only by our own company, not sold or listed.
    • Reads accounting reports only; never writes to QuickBooks.
    • Runs on our own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer, encrypted with Windows data protection. No data is sent to any server other than Intuit's.
    • Uses OAuth 2.0 and refreshes access tokens automatically. Intuit reviews this and then turns on production keys. Timing is up to Intuit.
  5. Add the redirect address. In the app, go to Settings > Redirect URIs > Production, click Add URI, enter exactly the address below, and click Save:

https://developer.intuit.com/v2/OAuth2Playground/RedirectUrl

This is Intuit's own page. After you approve a company, Intuit sends you there with a one-time code, which you paste into Claude (see Part 3). 6. Copy your keys. Go to Keys and credentials, Production tab. You'll need the Client ID and Client Secret in Part 2. Treat the Client Secret like a password: don't email it or paste it into a chat.

Part 2: Install the add-on in Claude (one time)

  1. Double-click QuickBooks-Multi-Company.mcpb. The Claude desktop app opens and offers to install it. Click Install.
  2. Claude asks for three settings: - Intuit Client ID: from Part 1, step 6. - Intuit Client Secret: from Part 1, step 6. Claude stores it securely. - Environment: leave it as production. (sandbox is only for Intuit's fake test companies.)
  3. Make sure it shows as enabled under Settings > Extensions. The first time it starts, it may take a minute or two to download the components it needs.

Part 3: Connect each company (a minute each)

Repeat these steps for each QuickBooks company:

  1. In a new Claude chat, type: Connect a QuickBooks company.
  2. Your web browser opens to QuickBooks. Sign in, pick the company and click Connect.
  3. You land on an Intuit page titled OAuth 2.0 Playground. Don't click anything on that page. Click the browser's address bar, copy the whole address (it starts with https://developer.intuit.com/v2/OAuth2Playground/RedirectUrl?code=), then paste it into Claude and press Enter.
  4. Claude replies "Connected ''."

You have 15 minutes to paste the address, and each code only works once. If anything goes wrong, just start again at step 1.

When all your companies are connected, ask: List my QuickBooks companies.

Part 4: Asking questions

Talk to Claude normally. Some examples:

  • "Compare October net income across all my companies."
  • "Which company has the most receivables over 60 days old?"
  • "Show each company's cash balance."
  • "Show the balance sheet for as of September 30."
  • "Line up year-to-date income and net income for every company."
  • "List unpaid invoices over $5,000 for ."

Tips:

  • You can refer to a company by part of its name. If the part matches more than one company, Claude will ask which one you mean.
  • Reports use accrual accounting unless you ask for cash basis.
  • Claude can add up and compare the numbers, but check anything important against QuickBooks itself, as you would with any report.

Part 5: Keeping it working

  • Reconnecting. Intuit cancels a company's access if it goes unused for about 100 days, and may sometimes ask you to sign in again anyway. When Claude says a company needs reconnecting, or a question about it fails, just repeat Part 3 for that company. "List my QuickBooks companies" shows how many days each connection has left.
  • Removing a company. Ask Claude: "Disconnect ." This deletes the saved sign-in on this computer and cancels the app's access at Intuit. Nothing inside QuickBooks is changed.
  • New Client Secret. If you reset your Client Secret at Intuit, enter the new one under Settings > Extensions > QuickBooks Multi-Company > Configure.
  • New computer or Windows user. Saved sign-ins only work for the Windows user who created them. On a new PC, install the add-on (Part 2) and reconnect each company (Part 3).
  • Where things are stored. %APPDATA%\QuickBooksMulti holds the encrypted sign-ins (tokens.enc) and the protected key that unlocks them (token.key). Deleting that folder removes all connections.

Part 6: Updating it yourself

You have the complete source code, so you can change it however you like.

Installing a newer version. Double-click the new .mcpb file and click Install (or Update). Your connected companies stay connected, because they're stored separately in %APPDATA%\QuickBooksMulti.

Making a change. The easiest way is to use Claude Code:

  1. Open the source folder in Claude Code and describe the change, for example: "Intuit changed the report format; fix the aging report," or "Add a tool for sales by customer."
  2. Ask Claude to raise the version number in manifest.json and rebuild the add-on. The build command is below; it needs Node.js, which Claude Code already uses:

npx @anthropic-ai/mcpb pack . QuickBooks-Multi-Company.mcpb

  1. Install the new .mcpb file as above.

Ground rule for any change: keep it read-only. src/qbo_mcp/guard.py is the part that blocks writes, and tests/test_guard.py proves it. Don't loosen either one.

If Intuit changes things. Intuit announces API changes on its developer blog (https://blog.developer.intuit.com). If questions start failing for every company, check that first, then ask Claude Code to look at the error.

Troubleshooting

What you see What to do
The add-on doesn't appear, or Claude says the tools aren't available Settings > Extensions: make sure it's enabled. Fully quit and reopen Claude.
"Missing Intuit Client ID" or "Client Secret" Settings > Extensions > QuickBooks Multi-Company > Configure, and fill them in.
Intuit shows an error about the redirect URI In your Intuit app, check that Settings > Redirect URIs > Production has exactly the address in Part 1, step 5.
"That sign-in is more than 15 minutes old" or "state mismatch" Start Part 3 again from step 1, and paste the address within 15 minutes.
"Refresh token ... is no longer valid" That company needs reconnecting: repeat Part 3.
A company doesn't appear in the QuickBooks company list You must be an admin in that company to connect apps.
Every company fails at once Check that your production keys are still active in the Intuit developer portal, and check the Intuit developer blog for API changes.
